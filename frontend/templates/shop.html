About us

The people behind
the knots.

The Happy Knots began with Rakhi’s love of making things by hand. Today, Rakhi and Aryan are building a place to share that creativity with more people.

Maker & founder

Rakhi Dixit

What started as a hobby became The Happy Knots. Rakhi makes the crochet pieces and looks forward to growing the business, one handmade creation at a time.

Co-founder · Marketing manager · Online executive

Aryan Dixit

Aryan leads the online side of The Happy Knots, from marketing to the customer experience. He is working to bring Rakhi’s handmade creations to more people.

Shipping Policy

We ship from Bengaluru, Karnataka to serviceable addresses across India. We use India Post or private courier services depending on the destination and the parcel.

Ready-to-ship orders

For ready-to-ship items, our estimated delivery times from the date you place your order are:

  • Bengaluru and nearby cities: 2–3 business days.
  • Northern and Eastern India: 7–8 business days.
  • Other serviceable destinations in India: generally 3–8 business days.

These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. Sundays and public holidays are not business days. Remote areas or courier disruptions may take longer.

Made-to-order pieces

Custom-made or handcrafted pieces that are not ready to ship may take approximately 1–2 weeks to make before dispatch. Delivery time then depends on the destination. We will let you know if an item needs more time.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free when the item subtotal is ₹1,000 or more. For orders below ₹1,000, shipping is ₹80. The checkout shows the shipping charge and total due on delivery before you place your order.

Serviceable addresses

We ship to most serviceable Indian PIN codes. If your address cannot be served by our available courier options, we will contact you before dispatch.

Dispatch and tracking

When your order is dispatched, we will share a shipment update and a tracking number if the carrier provides one. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to become active after dispatch.

Delays

High order volume, weather, holidays, and carrier issues may delay delivery. If we know of a significant delay, we will contact you using the email address or phone number on your order.

Damaged or missing parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, an item is missing, or the parcel is lost in transit, email h4appy.knots.official@gmail.com with your order details and photos of the item and packaging if available. An unboxing video can help us investigate but is not required to contact us. Please keep the packaging while we review the issue. See our returns and refunds policy for the next steps.

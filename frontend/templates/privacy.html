Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Welcome to The Happy Knots (“we”, “our”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit www.happyknots.shop, create an account, save a delivery address, place an order, or contact us.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we receive your verified email address and a unique account identifier from our sign-in provider. If you save an address or place an order, we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, and order details. We also receive information you choose to send us by email.

Our hosting and security services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and request details. We use a short-lived, hashed version of your IP address to limit repeated sign-in attempts.

2. How we collect information

We collect information when you sign in, save or remove an address, place an order, or email us. Some technical information is collected automatically when you use the website or its sign-in protection.

3. How we use information

We use your information to provide and secure your account, save your addresses, process and deliver orders, respond to questions, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, protect account access, and complete the order flow. Your shopping bag and cookie choices are saved in your browser. Google reCAPTCHA may use cookies or similar technology on the sign-in page to detect automated activity and protect customer accounts. These essential technologies cannot be switched off through our preference panel because the related features would not work without them.

We do not currently use advertising or visitor analytics cookies. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are added later, they will remain off unless you allow them. You can review or change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

5. Service providers

We use Render to host the website, MongoDB Atlas to store account profile and order data, Amazon Cognito for customer sign-in, Google reCAPTCHA to protect the sign-in flow, and Razorpay to process optional online payments. Razorpay receives the payment information needed to complete a transaction; Happy Knots does not receive or store card numbers, UPI PINs or online-banking credentials. We retain Razorpay order and payment identifiers with the shop order for confirmation, support and refunds. We may also share delivery details with a delivery provider when needed to fulfill an order.

6. Sharing of information

We share personal information only as needed to operate the website, fulfill orders, provide support, or comply with applicable law. We do not sell or rent customer personal information.

7. Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect personal information, including HTTPS for the public website and access controls for our services. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Data retention

We keep account, address, and order information for as long as needed to provide the service, respond to support requests, maintain order records, and meet applicable legal obligations. To request deletion or correction, contact us using the details below. Some order records may need to be retained where required by law.

9. Processing locations

The Happy Knots is based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Our service providers may process or store information in other countries where they operate.

10. Children’s privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and address it.

11. Your privacy choices

You can manage saved delivery addresses in your account. You can also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and record-keeping requirements.

12. Applicable privacy law

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws. This policy will be updated as our services or legal obligations change.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version and its updated date will be posted on this page.

Returns and Refunds

At The Happy Knots, every piece is handmade with care. Please read this policy before placing an order. Your rights under applicable consumer law are not limited by this policy.

When a return is available

Please contact us if your item arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially different from its description, or if a delivery is late in circumstances where applicable law gives you a remedy. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate return, replacement, or refund.

Change-of-mind returns

We generally do not accept returns because you changed your mind, ordered the wrong item, or prefer a different colour or design. Small variations in handmade pieces are expected and are not defects by themselves. Custom, personalized, sale, and promotional items are also generally not returnable for a change of mind. This does not affect remedies available for damaged, defective, incorrect, or otherwise non-conforming goods.

How to report a problem

Email h4appy.knots.official@gmail.com as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours of delivery. Include your order number, a short description, and clear photos of the item and packaging if available. An unboxing video can help us investigate, but it is not required to make a claim. We will still consider reports made after 48 hours in accordance with applicable law.

Return shipping

Please wait for our instructions before sending an item back. If we approve a return because an item was damaged, defective, or incorrect, we will arrange or cover reasonable return shipping. We will explain the return process by email.

Refunds and replacements

After reviewing an eligible claim, we will tell you whether a replacement or refund is appropriate. We do not offer preference-based exchanges. Store credit may be offered as an option, but you do not have to accept it in place of an eligible monetary refund.

Our website currently uses cash on delivery. If you paid for an order and a refund is approved, we will arrange a UPI or bank-transfer refund with you privately. We aim to process approved refunds within 5–10 business days; the time for funds to appear may depend on your bank or payment provider.

Order cancellation

To request cancellation, email us with your order number before dispatch. Once an order has been dispatched, we may be unable to cancel it, but your rights relating to damaged, incorrect, defective, or qualifying late goods still apply.

Shipping addresses

Please check your delivery address and phone number before placing an order. If you notice an error, contact us promptly so we can try to correct it before dispatch. We will explain any delivery issue or additional cost caused by an incorrect address before taking further action.
